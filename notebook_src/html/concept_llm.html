What is an LLM?
A next-token prediction machine
Given a sequence of text, an LLM assigns a probability to every possible next token and samples from that distribution.
"The capital of France is "

Key insight — LLMs don't look facts up in a database. They generate statistically likely continuations. A model can confidently produce "The Great Wall is visible from space" — because it sounds plausible — even though it's false. This is why grounding in a knowledge base matters.
