No single representation wins on every axis — that's why hybrid RAG (graph + vector) exists.
Most production knowledge systems are layered: free text for human review, a graph for audited multi-hop facts, and embeddings for fuzzy semantic recall — each covering the axis the others are weak on.
Key insight — Representation is a trade-off, not a ladder to "solve." Moving toward structure buys queryability and precision at the cost of expressiveness and readability — the right choice depends on what you need to ask of the knowledge, not which format looks most sophisticated.
