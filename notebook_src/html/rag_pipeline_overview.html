What is RAG?
The Retrieval-Augmented Generation Pipeline
Documents are extracted and stored as embeddings offline. At query time, the system searches those embeddings, retrieves matching chunks, and augments the LLM's generation with grounded facts.
now: information extraction
Key insight — RAG separates what the model knows (its parameters) from what it can access (the knowledge base). The quality of every answer is bounded by the quality of the retrieved context.
